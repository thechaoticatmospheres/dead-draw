Crew, campaign & saved runs

T / D-pad left opens objectives, door contributions, entrance repairs and traps. Z pings your location. C / LT + LS click shoves enemies. Share ammunition with nearby teammates. Downed players use [ / ] or Y / RB to spectate.

Restore Sapphire power, collect two boss keys, recover the Neon vault code and challenge the House in Eclipse. After victory, vote to extract in the atrium or continue endless waves. Safe intermissions save automatically; use Resume saved run after refreshing. Complete casino hands before leaving.

Choose Shared Crew Table at roulette, blackjack, baccarat or craps to play alongside friends. Cards and wheel wait for everyone's confirmation. At craps the shooter controls rolls. No intermission countdown.

Survival guide · The High Rollers update

Space / B dodges through danger and costs stamina. G / RS click throws a grenade; charges refill each wave. Consecutive kills within 4 seconds build a chip-bonus streak. Finish the crew contract for bonus chips and comps.

Runners close fast. Pit Bosses wind up a ground slam—leave the gold circle. Spitters hold back and leave acid pools—move before the green circle fills. Every fifth wave brings the High Roller, whose attacks accelerate below half health. Boss caches restore ammo, health and grenades.

Use U / D-pad → between rounds to open the Survivor’s Club. Spend chips on supplies and weapon upgrades, and casino comps on perks. New rooms unlock more club stock. All upgrades reset when a new run starts.
